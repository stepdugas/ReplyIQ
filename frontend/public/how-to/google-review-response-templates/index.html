Google Review Response Templates That Actually Work

Templates are a smart starting point for review responses — but only if you use them correctly. A generic "Thank you for your review!" copied and pasted across every response looks lazy and can actually hurt your reputation. Here are templates that work, organized by star rating, with guidance on how to make each one feel personal.

5-Star Review Templates

Five-star reviews are your best marketing material. Your response should reinforce the positive experience and encourage the customer to return.

  • "Thank you so much, [Name]! We're thrilled to hear that [specific thing they mentioned]. Our team works hard to [related value], and it means a lot to know it shows. We look forward to seeing you again!"
  • "[Name], this made our day! [Reference their specific compliment]. We appreciate you taking the time to share your experience — it helps other customers find us and motivates our team."

4-Star Review Templates

A 4-star review is great, but the missing star is an opportunity. Acknowledge the positive while gently inviting feedback on what could earn that fifth star.

  • "Thank you for the great review, [Name]! We're glad you enjoyed [specific positive]. We're always looking to improve — if there's anything that would have made it a 5-star experience, we'd love to hear about it."
  • "We appreciate the kind words, [Name]! [Reference their comment]. We noticed you held back one star and we'd genuinely love to know how we can earn it next time."

3-Star Review Templates

Three-star reviews are critical — the customer is on the fence. Your response can tip them toward becoming a loyal customer or losing them forever.

  • "Thank you for your honest feedback, [Name]. We're glad [positive aspect] met your expectations, and we take your comments about [concern] seriously. We'd love the chance to make your next visit a 5-star experience."
  • "We appreciate you sharing this, [Name]. Mixed experiences are the most valuable feedback we can get because they show us exactly where to improve. We're working on [relevant improvement] and hope to welcome you back soon."

1-2 Star Review Templates

Low-star reviews need the most careful handling. Stay professional, acknowledge the issue, and move the conversation offline.

  • "[Name], we're sorry to hear about your experience. This is not the standard we hold ourselves to. We'd like to learn more about what happened and make it right — please reach out to us at [contact info] so we can address this personally."
  • "Thank you for bringing this to our attention, [Name]. We take feedback like this seriously and want to resolve it. Please contact us directly at [contact info] and we'll do everything we can to make this right."

The Personalization Rule

Templates become dangerous when you copy and paste them without changes. Readers — and Google — notice when every response looks identical. For every response, change at least two things: reference something specific from the review, and vary your opening line. If a customer mentions your staff by name, include that name. If they mention a specific product or service, reference it.
