How to Manage Google Reviews for Multiple Locations

Managing Google reviews for one location is hard enough. When you have multiple locations, it becomes nearly impossible to keep up manually. Each location has its own review stream, its own customers, and its own reputation to protect.

The Multi-Location Review Problem

Every business location generates its own Google reviews independently. A franchise with 10 locations might get 50+ reviews per week across all locations. Checking each location's Google Business Profile separately, writing responses, and keeping track of which reviews you've handled is a full-time job.

Centralizing Review Management

The solution is a centralized dashboard that pulls reviews from all your locations into one place. Instead of logging into each Google Business Profile separately, you see everything in a single feed. You can filter by location, by star rating, or by response status.

Setting Up Multi-Location Management

  1. Connect all locations — With ReplyIQ, you connect your Google Business Profile once and all your locations are automatically imported. Each location appears in your sidebar with its own settings.
  2. Customize per location — Set different tone preferences for different locations. A casual cafe might use a friendly tone while a law office uses a professional one.
  3. Choose auto-post or approve per location — High-volume locations can use auto-post to keep up, while flagship locations can require manual approval.
  4. Monitor from one dashboard — See total reviews, response rate, and average rating across all locations at a glance.

Why Consistency Matters

Customers notice when one location responds to reviews and another doesn't. Inconsistent review management hurts your brand. Automated tools ensure every location gets the same attention regardless of how busy your team is.
