How to Respond to Negative Google Reviews

Negative reviews sting, but how you respond matters more than the review itself. A thoughtful response to a 1-star review can actually increase trust — 45% of consumers say they're more likely to visit a business that responds to negative reviews.

The Golden Rules

  • Respond quickly — The longer a negative review sits unanswered, the worse it looks. Aim for within 24 hours.
  • Don't get defensive — Even if the review is unfair, arguing publicly never works. Other customers are watching how you handle conflict.
  • Acknowledge the problem — Show that you heard them. "I'm sorry your experience didn't meet expectations" goes a long way.
  • Take it offline — Offer to resolve the issue privately. "Please reach out to us at [email] so we can make this right."
  • Keep it short — Long defensive responses look worse than short empathetic ones.

Response Templates by Star Rating

1-Star Reviews

These require the most care. Acknowledge the frustration, apologize for the specific issue they mentioned, and offer to resolve it. Never blame the customer, even if they're wrong.

2-Star Reviews

Similar to 1-star, but the customer usually acknowledges something positive. Reference what they liked, then address the concern. Show you're working on it.

3-Star Reviews

These are opportunities. The customer is on the fence. Thank them for the feedback, acknowledge what could be better, and express genuine interest in improving their next visit.
